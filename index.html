What we do

How it works

Four steps, no guesswork

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Send the enquiry form or email us with what needs doing. A couple of photos help us understand the job quickly.

  2. 2

    Free visit & written quote

    We look at the job in person, then send a written quote that breaks down labour and materials separately. No obligation.

  3. 3

    Scope, price and dates agreed

    Once you approve the quote, we confirm a start date in writing and keep you updated as the work progresses.

  4. 4

    Snag list & sign-off

    We walk the job with you, put right anything on your list, clear the site, and only then call it finished.
