About IROCLEAR SOLUTIONS

A building business that is easy to deal with.

Most problems people have with trades are not about the workmanship. They are about the silence. IROCLEAR SOLUTIONS is set up to remove that: clear quotes, straight answers, and a crew that finishes what it starts.

Why we work this way

Construction and home improvement has a reputation problem, and most of it is earned. Quotes that quietly grow. Start dates that slip without a phone call. Sites left for somebody else to clear up.

We run this business the opposite way. Every job gets a written quote that itemises labour and materials before anyone lifts a tool. If we uncover something the quote did not cover, work stops and you get a call — not a surprise invoice at the end. And when we pack up, the site is left the way we would want to find it.

We take on work across the local area for homeowners, landlords and small businesses, and we would rather turn down a job we cannot do well than take it on and deliver something ordinary.

What we stand for

Four rules we do not bend

Clarity before cost

You see the price, broken down, before work begins. If we find something unexpected, we stop and talk it through with you before spending your money.

Do it once, properly

We would rather spend an extra hour now than come back to the same fault in six months. Shortcuts cost you more in the long run.

Respect the space

We work in places where people live and run businesses. Dust sheets down, noise to sensible hours, waste taken away, tools locked up.

Say it straight

If a job is not worth doing, or is not one we should take on, we will tell you. A short honest answer beats a long polite one.

Who we work with

Three kinds of client

Homeowners

One room or several, a repair that has waited long enough, or a renovation you have been planning for years. We work around your household, not through it.

Landlords

Fast, tidy turnarounds between tenants, and repairs logged and completed with a written record you can file away.

Small businesses

Shopfitting repairs, office maintenance and planned work scheduled outside your busiest trading hours.

Where we work

We are based locally and cover the surrounding towns and villages. If you are not sure whether you fall inside our area, send your postcode with your enquiry and we will tell you straight away — and if we cannot help, we will say so.
